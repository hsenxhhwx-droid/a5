Our Story

Made because a bag shouldn't sag by winter

We're a small, independent studio — not a large manufacturer. Here's the plain version of how we got here.

Where It Started

A bag that looked beautiful and lasted one season

Velvet Purse Bay started with a specific frustration: a handbag that looked wonderful in the store and had visibly sagged and cracked within a year of normal use. We began sourcing full-grain leather directly and testing construction ourselves, checking how seams and hardware actually held up under daily wear rather than trusting a showroom impression.

Every bag since has been built around that same tested standard, not just a finished look.

How We Work

A few habits we hold to on every bag

01

Full-grain leather, checked by hand

Every hide is inspected for genuine full-grain quality before it's cut.

02

Hardware tested for years, not months

Zippers and clasps are chosen for how they perform after years of daily use.

03

Small batches, finished properly

We'd rather finish fewer bags well than rush a larger run.
